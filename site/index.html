Get Buddy going

About two minutes.

Download it. Mac: unzip and drag The Stupid Buddy into Applications (first time: right-click → Open → Open). Windows: run the installer (if Windows warns you, click More info → Run anyway). Android: open the APK on your phone and allow the install.

Choose a brain. Free online: get a key at Groq or Google and paste it in Buddy’s Settings. Or on your computer: install Ollama and run ollama pull qwen3-vl:8b-instruct.

Talk to Buddy. On a Mac or PC, click the ball in the corner of your screen. On a phone, just open the app (and fling Buddy’s ball around if you like).
